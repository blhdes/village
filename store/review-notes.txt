VILLE has no accounts and no sign-in.

To try it: tap the people icon (two figures) in the top bar, type a Letterboxd username such as 812filmreviews and tap "Search". When the member appears, tap "Read their reviews" to read them as reels first, or "Follow". You can also tap "Follow" next to anyone in the "Suggested" list, or tap their name to read them first. Close the sheet: the recent written reviews of the members you follow appear as full-screen reels. Swipe up to read. After the session's last review a "Fin" card appears, with "Keep reading". The sliders icon in the top bar opens Settings: appearance, posters, backdrops (off by default) and ratings, reading (session length, showing each review only once, hiding very short reviews), language, storage, and About (privacy policy, help and contact).

Content: VILLE only shows reviews from the public RSS feed Letterboxd publishes for each member (letterboxd.com/<username>/rss/), and only for members the user follows or searches for, one at a time. Every review credits its author and links to the original on Letterboxd ("View on Letterboxd"). Tapping the author's name ("Review by ...") on any reel opens a menu with "Report on Letterboxd", which opens that review on Letterboxd, where content is reported, and, in the user's feed, "Unfollow", which removes the member and their reviews from the device after a confirmation. Members can also be unfollowed in the people screen. Reviews the user bookmarked stay in Bookmarks. Film images come from the TMDB API, credited in Settings. No data leaves the device except these read-only requests.

VILLE has no accounts and no sign-in.

To try it: tap the people icon (two figures) in the top bar and add any member from the "Suggested" list with its "Add" button, or type a Letterboxd username such as 812filmreviews. Close the sheet: their recent written reviews appear as full-screen reels. Swipe up to read. After the session's last review a "Fin" card appears, with "Keep reading". The sliders icon in the top bar opens Settings: appearance, posters, backdrops (off by default) and ratings, reading (session length, showing each review only once, hiding very short reviews), language, storage, and About (privacy policy, help and contact).

Content: VILLE only shows reviews from the public RSS feed Letterboxd publishes for each member (letterboxd.com/<username>/rss/), and only for members the user adds. Every review credits its author and links to the original on Letterboxd ("View on Letterboxd"). Tapping the author's name ("Review by ...") on any review opens a menu with "Report on Letterboxd", which opens that review on Letterboxd, where content is reported, and "Unfollow", which removes the member and their reviews from the device after a confirmation. Members can also be removed in the people screen. Reviews the user bookmarked stay in Bookmarks. Film images come from the TMDB API, credited in Settings. No data leaves the device except these read-only requests.

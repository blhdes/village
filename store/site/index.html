Reviews, one at a time

VILLE turns the film reviews of the Letterboxd members you choose into full-screen reels: one per page, and a feed with an end.

Questions

How do I add someone?
Tap the people icon in the top bar and type a Letterboxd username or paste a profile link. Or tap + next to someone in Suggested.
Why don't I see some of their reviews?
VILLE shows written reviews only. Lists and diary entries without text are left out. Each member's public feed holds their latest entries (about 50), and VILLE keeps every review it has saved, even after it leaves the feed.
How do I get new reviews?
Pull the first reel down. VILLE also checks again when you come back after 30 minutes or more, and starts a new session.
Can I read the review before seeing the rating?
Yes: tap the sliders icon in the top bar and turn Stars and likes off. You can also switch posters, alternative posters and backdrops on or off, change how many reviews a session holds, and pick light, dark or system appearance.
Can I use VILLE in another language?
VILLE comes in English, Spanish, German, French, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese and Chinese, and follows your iPhone. To pick another, tap the sliders icon, then Language. A review written in another language shows Translate from… under its date: one tap translates it, on your iPhone.
How do I remove someone?
In the people screen, swipe left on their name. Their reviews are deleted from your iPhone too.
Does VILLE collect my data?
No. There's no account and nothing leaves your iPhone except the requests that fetch reviews and film art. See the privacy policy.

En español

VILLE convierte las reseñas de cine de los miembros de Letterboxd que eliges en reels a pantalla completa: una por página, y con final.

Para añadir a alguien, toca el icono de personas de la barra superior y escribe su nombre de usuario de Letterboxd (o pega el enlace de su perfil). Para buscar reseñas nuevas, desliza hacia abajo la primera reseña. Para quitar a alguien, desliza su nombre hacia la izquierda en esa misma pantalla. Ajustes como las estrellas, los pósteres o la duración de la sesión están en el icono de ajustes (los deslizadores). Ahí también puedes cambiar el idioma de la app, en Idioma. Si una reseña está en otro idioma, toca Traducir del… bajo su fecha y se traduce en tu iPhone.
